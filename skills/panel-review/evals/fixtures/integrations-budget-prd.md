# Q3 Integrations Budget Proposal

## Summary

The integrations team is requesting $200K for Q3 to accelerate connector
coverage. With this investment we expect ARR to grow 3x over the next 18
months, driven by enterprise deals that are currently stalled on missing
integrations.

## Why now

Our top 3 enterprise prospects have all cited the lack of native Salesforce,
Workday, and NetSuite connectors as a blocker. Each of these deals is worth
~$400K ARR. Without acceleration we'll lose them to a competitor that ships
all three out of the box.

## What we'll build

The $200K covers two contractors at $80K each over the quarter plus $40K of
infrastructure (CI capacity, partner-program fees, sandbox environments).
By end of Q3 we will have shipped Salesforce, Workday, and NetSuite
connectors, each with bidirectional sync and OAuth.

## How we'll measure

- 3 connectors shipped by end of Q3
- 2 of the 3 stalled enterprise deals closed by end of Q4
- Net-new ARR of $800K attributable to the connectors by end of Q1 next year

## Risk

Hiring 2 contractors in two weeks is tight; our usual cycle is six. If we
slip the contractor start by a month we still ship 2 of 3 connectors. The
third (NetSuite) would slip to early Q4.

## Decision needed

Approval to spend $200K from the Q3 product budget by Friday so we can sign
contractor offers Monday.
